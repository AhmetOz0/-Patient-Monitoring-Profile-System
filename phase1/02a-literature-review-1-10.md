# Chapter 2 · Literature Review (1–10) — Remote monitoring and usability

To meet the report template’s requirement of “at least 20 research papers from 2014 to the present”, 20 studies were reviewed; this file contains the first 10. For each paper the method, finding, limitation and effect on this design are stated separately.

### [1] · 2015 · Monitoring
**Effects of Home Telemonitoring Interventions on Patients With Chronic Heart Failure: An Overview of Systematic Reviews**
Kitsiou S, Paré G, Jaana M · Journal of Medical Internet Research, 17(3), e63

- **Method:** High-level synthesis of 15 systematic reviews; evidence assessed with AMSTAR and GRADE.
- **Finding:** Positive results were found for some types of home monitoring; effectiveness varied by technology and patient group.
- **Limitation:** Heterogeneity between studies and low/moderate evidence quality for some outcomes limit firm generalisation.
- **Relevance to the project:** Measurement history and the clinician’s assessment are handled together; no clinical success claim is added to the prototype.
- DOI: [10.2196/jmir.4174](https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.4174)

### [2] · 2017 · Monitoring
**Remote Monitoring of Patients With Heart Failure: An Overview of Systematic Reviews**
Bashi N, Karunanithi M, Fatehi F, Ding H, Walters D · Journal of Medical Internet Research, 19(1), e18

- **Method:** Review of 19 systematic reviews; comparison of different remote monitoring interventions.
- **Finding:** Supportive findings were seen for home telehealth and telemonitoring, while more research was called for on some mobile/video applications.
- **Limitation:** The interventions in the reviews are not the same; they do not prove the success of a single web application.
- **Relevance to the project:** This assignment is limited to record review and task delivery within one department.
- DOI: [10.2196/jmir.6571](https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.6571)

### [3] · 2017 · Monitoring
**Remote Patient Monitoring via Non-Invasive Digital Technologies: A Systematic Review**
Vegesna A, Tran M, Angelaccio M, Arcona S · Telemedicine and e-Health, 23(1), 3–17

- **Method:** EMBASE and MEDLINE search; 62 studies evaluated by technology and outcomes.
- **Finding:** Multi-component monitoring applications are common; positive findings were reported in many studies.
- **Limitation:** Small samples, short follow-up and varied outcome measures limit conclusions about scalability. Published online in 2016, volume year 2017.
- **Relevance to the project:** Instead of sensor integration, a testable baseline prototype with manual data entry is chosen.
- DOI: [10.1089/tmj.2016.0051](https://doi.org/10.1089/tmj.2016.0051)

### [4] · 2018 · Monitoring
**Impact of remote patient monitoring on clinical outcomes: an updated meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials**
Noah B, Keller MS, Mosadeghi S, et al. · npj Digital Medicine, 1, 20172

- **Method:** Review of 27 randomised trials on wearable biosensors and meta-analysis of selected outcomes.
- **Finding:** No statistically significant effect was found across the six pooled clinical outcomes; interventions differ markedly.
- **Limitation:** Biosensor-focused results are not a performance measure of department software with manual entry.
- **Relevance to the project:** Success criteria are correct records, rejection of unauthorised access and workflow completion rather than disease improvement.
- DOI: [10.1038/s41746-017-0002-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41746-017-0002-4)

### [5] · 2019 · Monitoring
**Remote patient monitoring: a comprehensive study**
Malasinghe LP, Ramzan N, Dahal K · Journal of Ambient Intelligence and Humanized Computing, 10, 57–76

- **Method:** Comprehensive technical review of contact and contactless remote monitoring technologies.
- **Finding:** The physiological data to be measured and the application setting determine the monitoring technology used.
- **Limitation:** A broad technology survey; it offers no implementation experiment for this six-table schema. Online 2017, volume year 2019.
- **Relevance to the project:** Measurement type and unit are kept in a separate catalogue; sensor hardware is out of scope.
- DOI: [10.1007/s12652-017-0598-x](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12652-017-0598-x)

### [6] · 2024 · Monitoring
**A systematic review of the impacts of remote patient monitoring (RPM) interventions on safety, adherence, quality-of-life and cost-related outcomes**
Tan SY, Sumner J, Wang Y, Yip AW · npj Digital Medicine, 7, 192

- **Method:** Systematic review of monitoring interventions in the transition from hospital to home.
- **Finding:** Positive findings were reported for safety and adherence; other clinical and quality-of-life outcomes were more variable.
- **Limitation:** It is hard to separate the effects of technology and human support; settings and interventions are heterogeneous.
- **Relevance to the project:** Showing data and assigning an action to a clinician are designed as separate but linked functions.
- DOI: [10.1038/s41746-024-01182-w](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41746-024-01182-w)

### [7] · 2021 · Monitoring
**Does remote patient monitoring reduce acute care use? A systematic review**
Taylor ML, Thomas EE, Snoswell CL, Smith AC, Caffery LJ · BMJ Open, 11(3), e040232

- **Method:** Narrative synthesis of studies on acute care use across different disease and device groups.
- **Finding:** Use decreased in some studies and did not change in a large share; monitoring and staff communication methods differ.
- **Limitation:** The diversity of studies makes it hard to reduce them to one common effectiveness result.
- **Relevance to the project:** The person responsible for a message and its completion result are kept explicit; reduced care use is not claimed as an outcome of this project.
- DOI: [10.1136/bmjopen-2020-040232](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2020-040232)

### [8] · 2021 · Monitoring
**The Effect of Noninvasive Telemonitoring for Chronic Heart Failure on Health Care Utilization: Systematic Review**
Auener SL, Remers TEP, van Dulmen SA, Westert GP, Kool RB, Jeurissen PPT · Journal of Medical Internet Research, 23(9), e26744

- **Method:** Systematic review of noninvasive monitoring programmes in heart failure, focusing on health care use and costs.
- **Finding:** Resource use and especially cost results are inconsistent; some studies report cost increases and others decreases.
- **Limitation:** Programme content and cost measurement differ; it cannot be concluded that a simple record system will save money.
- **Relevance to the project:** In the industry benefits section, expected process benefits are separated from proven economic benefit.
- DOI: [10.2196/26744](https://doi.org/10.2196/26744)

### [9] · 2015 · Usability
**Impact of mHealth Chronic Disease Management on Treatment Adherence and Patient Outcomes: A Systematic Review**
Hamine S, Gerth-Guyette E, Faulx D, Green BB, Ginsburg AS · Journal of Medical Internet Research, 17(2), e52

- **Method:** Review of mobile health, treatment adherence, usability and clinical outcomes in 107 publications.
- **Finding:** The feasibility of the tools is promising; effects on adherence and clinical outcomes are mixed.
- **Limitation:** Technology alone does not replace the care process; studies differ in design and quality.
- **Relevance to the project:** Forms are kept short, units are shown clearly and error messages point to the field to correct.
- DOI: [10.2196/jmir.3951](https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.3951)

### [10] · 2021 · Usability
**Remote Monitoring Systems for Patients With Chronic Diseases in Primary Health Care: Systematic Review**
Peyroteo M, Ferreira IA, Elvas LB, Ferreira JC, Lapão LV · JMIR mHealth and uHealth, 9(12), e28285

- **Method:** Review of 18 publications on chronic disease monitoring systems in primary care.
- **Finding:** Fitting into existing information systems and professionals’ workflows is a key implementation challenge.
- **Limitation:** The primary care context differs from a hospital department; the small number of studies limits generalisation.
- **Relevance to the project:** A single department, clear roles and messages linked to the patient profile reduce the learning effort.
- DOI: [10.2196/28285](https://doi.org/10.2196/28285)

**Effects of Home Telemonitoring Interventions on Patients With Chronic Heart Failure: An Overview of Systematic Reviews**
Kitsiou S, Paré G, Jaana M · Journal of Medical Internet Research, 17(3), e63

- **Bulgu:** Bazı evde izleme türlerinde olumlu sonuçlar bulunmuştur; etkinlik teknolojiye ve hasta grubuna göre değişmiştir.

- **Projedeki karşılığı:** Ölçüm geçmişi ve sağlık çalışanının değerlendirmesi birlikte ele alınır; prototipe klinik başarı iddiası eklenmez.

### [2] · 2017 · İzleme
**Remote Monitoring of Patients With Heart Failure: An Overview of Systematic Reviews**
Bashi N, Karunanithi M, Fatehi F, Ding H, Walters D · Journal of Medical Internet Research, 19(1), e18

- **Yöntem:** 19 sistematik derlemenin incelenmesi; farklı uzaktan izleme müdahalelerinin karşılaştırılması.
- **Bulgu:** Evde tele-sağlık ve tele-izleme için destekleyici bulgular görülürken bazı mobil/video uygulamaları için daha fazla araştırma gerektiği belirtilmiştir.
- **Sınırlılık:** Derlemelerdeki müdahaleler aynı değildir; tek bir web uygulamasının başarısını kanıtlamaz.
- **Projedeki karşılığı:** Bu ödev tek bir bölümde kayıt inceleme ve görev iletimiyle sınırlandırılır.
- DOI: [10.2196/jmir.6571](https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.6571)

### [3] · 2017 · İzleme
**Remote Patient Monitoring via Non-Invasive Digital Technologies: A Systematic Review**
Vegesna A, Tran M, Angelaccio M, Arcona S · Telemedicine and e-Health, 23(1), 3–17

- **Yöntem:** EMBASE ve MEDLINE taraması; 62 çalışmanın teknoloji ve sonuçlar açısından değerlendirilmesi.
- **Bulgu:** Çok bileşenli izleme uygulamaları yaygındır; birçok çalışmada olumlu bulgular raporlanmıştır.
- **Sınırlılık:** Küçük örneklemler, kısa takip ve sonuç ölçütlerinin çeşitliliği ölçeklenebilirlik yorumlarını sınırlar. Erken çevrim içi yayın 2016, cilt yılı 2017’dir.
- **Projedeki karşılığı:** Sensör entegrasyonu yerine elle veri girişli, sınanabilir bir temel prototip seçilir.
- DOI: [10.1089/tmj.2016.0051](https://doi.org/10.1089/tmj.2016.0051)

### [4] · 2018 · İzleme
**Impact of remote patient monitoring on clinical outcomes: an updated meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials**
Noah B, Keller MS, Mosadeghi S, et al. · npj Digital Medicine, 1, 20172

- **Yöntem:** Giyilebilir biyosensörleri inceleyen 27 randomize çalışmanın derlenmesi ve seçilmiş sonuçların meta-analizi.
- **Bulgu:** Birleştirilen altı klinik ölçütte istatistiksel olarak anlamlı bir etki bulunmamıştır; müdahaleler belirgin biçimde farklıdır.
- **Sınırlılık:** Biyosensör odaklı sonuçlar, elle kayıt girilen bölüm içi yazılımın performans ölçümü değildir.
- **Projedeki karşılığı:** Başarı ölçütleri hastalık iyileşmesi yerine doğru kayıt, yetkisiz erişimin reddi ve iş akışının tamamlanmasıdır.
- DOI: [10.1038/s41746-017-0002-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41746-017-0002-4)

### [5] · 2019 · İzleme
**Remote patient monitoring: a comprehensive study**
Malasinghe LP, Ramzan N, Dahal K · Journal of Ambient Intelligence and Humanized Computing, 10, 57–76

- **Yöntem:** Temaslı ve temassız uzaktan izleme teknolojilerinin kapsamlı teknik incelemesi.
- **Bulgu:** Ölçülecek fizyolojik veri ve uygulama ortamı, kullanılan izleme teknolojisini belirler.
- **Sınırlılık:** Geniş bir teknoloji araştırmasıdır; bu altı tabloluk şema için bir uygulama deneyi sunmaz. Çevrim içi 2017, cilt yılı 2019’dur.
- **Projedeki karşılığı:** Ölçüm türü ve birim ayrı katalogda tutulur; sensör donanımı proje kapsamına alınmaz.
- DOI: [10.1007/s12652-017-0598-x](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12652-017-0598-x)

### [6] · 2024 · İzleme
